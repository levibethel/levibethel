CREATIVE SERVICES AGREEMENT (CSA)

GOVERNING LAW: NEW JERSEY

This Creative Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into by and between Fermion Bec Productions, a New Jersey limited liability company (“Producer”), and [Client Organization Name] (“Client”).

1. Scope of Work & Deliverables

Producer agrees to perform the creative production services and produce the final deliverables specified in the accompanying SOW. Any addition, modification, or expansion beyond the agreed Deliverables will be governed by a written Change Order signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

2. Invoicing, Payment Terms & Collections

Client agrees to pay Producer all Fees and approved Costs in accordance with Option A: 3-Part Milestone Billing (30% / 40% / 30%). Invoices are issued via QuickBooks Online and are Due Upon Receipt. Unpaid balances past thirty (30) days accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month (18% per annum) or the maximum permitted by law. In the event legal action is required to collect overdue payments, Client shall pay all reasonable attorney’s fees, court costs, and collection expenses incurred by Producer.

3. Revision Boundaries & Change Orders

All packages include two (2) consolidated stakeholder revision rounds. Client feedback must be consolidated and delivered within five (5) business days of cut delivery. Supplemental re-cuts, changes departing from approved scripts/storyboards after physical production wrap, or additional aspect-ratio deliverables are billed cleanly at fixed post-production rates ($150/hour or $850/post-production day) or applicable crew day rates if re-shoots are necessitated.

4. Production Cancellation, Postponement & Weather Days

Because physical production requires reserving specialized personnel, equipment packages, and locations: (a) Notice provided 8+ business days prior to call time: Client is responsible for 100% of non-refundable hard costs incurred; deposit balance applied to rescheduled date. (b) Notice provided 3 to 7 business days prior: Client is responsible for 50% of scheduled production labor fees plus 100% of non-recoverable costs. (c) Notice provided less than 72 hours prior: Client is responsible for 100% of scheduled crew day rates and committed rental/location expenses. (d) Weather Day Policy: If adverse weather prevents safe outdoor filming, the shoot day may be rescheduled upon mutual agreement; Client agrees to pay a Weather Day Fee equal to 50% of the daily crew rate plus direct equipment extension costs.

5. Intellectual Property, Rights Transfer & Raw Footage

Contingent upon Producer’s receipt of full and final payment of all Fees and Costs, Producer assigns to Client all worldwide copyright, title, and interest in and to the final, rendered Deliverables. All preliminary concepts, outtakes, raw camera footage (BRAW, ProRes, or RAW rushes), audio stems, DaVinci Resolve project files (.drp), and proprietary color grading node trees remain the exclusive intellectual property and preliminary works of Producer. Raw camera footage and working project files are excluded from final Deliverables and require a separate archival buyout agreement. Producer reserves the perpetual, royalty-free right to display finished work, behind-the-scenes assets, and Client’s name/logo across Producer’s website, reels, and promotional platforms.

6. Limitation of Liability

Except concerning each party’s indemnification obligations, neither party will be liable for incidental, consequential, indirect, or special damages (including lost profits or business interruption). Fermion Bec Productions’s total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total cash Fees actually paid by Client to Fermion Bec Productions under the applicable SOW.

7. Governing Law & Dispute Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New Jersey. Exclusive venue for any dispute arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be in the state or federal courts located in Monmouth County or Ocean County, New Jersey.
